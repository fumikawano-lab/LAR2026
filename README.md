# Supplemental files
- **individual_input_data**: JSON-formatted individual experimental data, including 26 male and 26 female mice
- **individual_network**: image files of individual-specific network obtained from BVN inference
- **python_codes**: full codes for inferring using depression and brain BVN model
  * Please see the [original BVN repository](https://github.com/fumikawano-lab/Bioreaction-Variation-Network) for the pipeline of pre-training and for downloading the pre-trained model.
---
- **All community data.xlsx**: full data of community components and their centralities shown in Fig. 2E and 2F
- **community top10 strength.xlsx**: details of top 10 nodes defined by strength (sum of correlation coefficient)
- **Primer sequences.xlsx**: showing the primer sequences for gene expression analysis
- **Summed centralities in input-containing sub-graphs.xlsx**: original data of heatmaps in Fig. 3 and Fig. 4
- **Supplemental Table S1**: individual data in Euclidean distance between the input features and predicted features in BVN inference
- **Supplemental Figure S1**: training loss progression during pre-training of depression and brain BVN model
- **Supplemental Figure S2**: validation of community classification using different thresholds for Spearman’s correlation coefficient and Jaccard index
- **Supplemental Figure S3**: running distance and its relationship to post-exercise plasma corticosterone in forced acute exercise test
- **Supplemental Figure S4**: mean running-wheel revolutions and its relationship to change in immobility time in chronic restraint stress plus voluntary exercise
- **Supplemental Figure S5**: heatmaps showing the summed centrality of input-containing sub-graphs within each community obtained by BVN inference after excluding immobility time
- **Supplemental Figure S6**: validation of BVN inference dependence on input data structure using representative individuals
